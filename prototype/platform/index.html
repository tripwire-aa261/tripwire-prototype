◎ Tripwire platform prototype v1.0

One sensor, three signals, five products

Tripwire sensors only listen. They hear drone echoes in signals already in the air (cell towers and digital TV), find GPS jammers, and point a heat camera at the drone. This prototype simulates the platform at seven real sites, from a prison fence to an Air Force flightline, a border crossing and a park during an event, and costs every product from a parts list.

Seven sites, three kinds of deployment

The fewest sensors that meet the default requirements (90% of the zone covered, a track 80% of the time, 25 m accuracy, the typical drone tracked 300 m before the fence) for a 0.9 kg drone at today's 20 dB of interference removal. "Cell only" is what the platform sensor does if digital TV does not work out.

        Five products from one sensor

        Each product is the same sensor with what it needs around it. Our cost comes from the parts list and the twin's sensor counts; the price is the financial model v2 plan price; the benchmark is the cheapest comparable system we found.

        GPS jammer locator

        The same sensors listen on GPS L1 through their 4-element GPS array. A jammer raises the noise at every sensor; differences in arrival time and the bearings place it.
