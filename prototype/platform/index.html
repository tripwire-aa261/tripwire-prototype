◎ Tripwire platform prototype v1.0

One sensor, three signals, five products

Tripwire sensors only listen. They hear drone echoes in signals already in the air (cell towers and digital TV), find GPS jammers, and point a heat camera at the drone. This prototype simulates the platform at seven real sites, from a prison fence to an Air Force flightline, a border crossing and a park during an event, and costs every product from a parts list.

Seven sites, three kinds of deployment

The fewest sensors that meet the default requirements (90% of the zone covered, a track 80% of the time, 25 m accuracy, the typical drone tracked 300 m before the fence), and how much interference removal that takes: 20 dB is what processing achieves today (ASSUMPTION), 30 and 40 dB are what better processing would add (not yet shown).

Cell towers alone. The cell-tower physics are the same as in the v0.5 twin, and the 5 kg results match it (Hays 4 sensors today, Fulton and Clemson at 30 dB, Hancock at 40 dB). v0.5 led with the 5 kg drone; this page also shows the 0.9 kg drone, which the twin gives a tenth of the echo (0.01 m² against 0.1 m², ASSUMPTIONS). Cell towers alone still find it at Hays today and at Fulton and Clemson with 40 dB of interference removal. The flightline (48 ha, four times Hays) and the port (28 ha) are too large for 24 cell-only sensors with the smaller drone. Digital TV adds stronger transmitters with wider coverage, which is why fewer sensors meet the requirements with it; that TV performance is an estimate until it is measured at Seed.

          Five products from one sensor

          Each product is the same sensor with what it needs around it. Our cost comes from the parts list and the twin's sensor counts; the price is the financial model v2 plan price; the benchmark is the cheapest comparable system we found.

          GPS jammer locator

          The same sensors listen on GPS L1 through their 4-element GPS array. A jammer raises the noise at every sensor; differences in arrival time and the bearings place it.
