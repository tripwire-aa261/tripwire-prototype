## Decisions in force

- **Seed = off-the-shelf radios** (bladeRF; B210 fallback); our own 8-channel receiver at Series A. Reason: prove the technology with buyable parts.
- **One fixed sensor build per radio**, hover held in software by **background history** (no microphone). Reason: matches or beats the microphone wherever the radar works.
- **Costs come from the twin** (`twin/cost.py` is the single source). Reason: numbers must be traceable.
- **Margins 40 % / 40 %; cost + margin is the price floor**; value-based fixed yearly subscription per facility recommended, all-in option, not per-alert. Reason: at the floor a facility earns ~$3-4k/yr.
- **Custom receiver NRE ($400k) is a company cost**, not in any unit price.
- **Compute:** edge GPU board per sensor + one on-site hub per facility.
- **Customer requirements** (drone, area, time tracked, 25 m accuracy, 300 m detection range for the typical drone) drive the recommendation and the Financials sensor count.
- **Prisons: low poles (~6 m)** on the fence; customer provides poles, power and network (not priced).
- **NEW. Stadiums: sensors alternate between the top of the stands and 6 m poles 20 m outside**; microphones off (crowd noise). Reason: placement study (`tools/placement_study.py`).
- **NEW. Stadium positioning: the bowl layer next to an RF detector.** Reason: the FAA restriction (3 NM) and the pilot's location are RF-detector strengths.
- **Interference removal stays out of the Overview**; competitor comparison like for like per facility.
- **Plain language** ("sensor", never "node"); every number cited or labeled ASSUMPTION; no secrets or papers in git.
- Commits go straight to main when there are no conflicts (Nimalan's call).
